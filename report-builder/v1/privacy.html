Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

HTI Report Builder is a client-side application. We do not store your report data, student names, or IDs on any server. All processing happens locally in your browser.

2. Image Processing

Images uploaded to the builder are processed locally. If you use an external image URL, it is fetched directly from the source to your browser.

3. Third-Party Services

We use hCaptcha for bot protection and Google Fonts for typography. These services may collect standard technical data as per their own privacy policies.

4. Local Storage

The application may use local storage to save your preferences (such as language settings) for a better user experience.
